# For AI readers

1. The claims are Theorem 1a and Corollary 1b in [README.md](README.md). The exact list, with evidence types and statuses, is [packet/claims.json](packet/claims.json).
2. Theorem 1a: the formal version in [statement/Challenge2.lean](statement/Challenge2.lean) (`ClaudeChallenge2.main`) is Lean-proved (the statement file ends in an intentional `sorry`; the proof is [packet/formal/Formal/Claude3ComparatorSolution.lean](packet/formal/Formal/Claude3ComparatorSolution.lean)). It has weaker hypotheses than the paper, so it is a stronger theorem. What a reader must check is (a) that this file says what the paper says, and (b) the prose correspondence lemma after Theorem 1a in the paper, which carries the paper's geometric hypotheses to the formal hypotheses.
3. Corollary 1b for K3, C1, O2 and D14 is Lean-proved with no prose bridge ([statement/ChallengeC1.lean](statement/ChallengeC1.lean) for C1, with its classification; [statement/ChallengeO2.lean](statement/ChallengeO2.lean) for O2, with absence for t, d ≥ 2; [statement/ChallengeD14.lean](statement/ChallengeD14.lean) for D14). For K3: [statement/ChallengeK3.lean](statement/ChallengeK3.lean) (`ClaudeChallengeK3.main`, statement only; proof in [packet/formal/Formal/Claude4K3ComparatorSolution.lean](packet/formal/Formal/Claude4K3ComparatorSolution.lean)) defines K3(r,s) directly. Check that its K3 is the paper's K3. The K3 closed classification is also Lean-proved (`Claude4K3.k3_statement`).
4. The Lean declarations listed in [ci/Axioms.lean](ci/Axioms.lean) are Theorem 1a, its residue-class variant, the K3, C1, O2 and D14 results, and the family arithmetic. Re-run them with the commands in README.md.
5. Detailed reading order: [packet/AGENTS.md](packet/AGENTS.md).
6. If you find an error, see [packet/P006-corrections.md](packet/P006-corrections.md) and open an issue.
