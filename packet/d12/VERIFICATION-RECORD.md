## Roles, alternative proof routes and independence

Route A was supplied by a AI worker after the research team's W/X
observations and boundary analysis. The formula and start-count predictions
were fixed before later tests. The Study research AI reproduced its
symbolic recipe and wrote an eager finite constructor and definition-based
search after reading supplied code. That same-agent alternative check is
not blind third-party review or a verified universal executor.

Route B was developed by the RouteB verification AI from its own model
and known statement without reading A's D12 construction, proof or code.
It supplies necessity and sufficiency separately. The routes are
alternative universal arguments, not combined premises. B's independence
note discloses shared-memory exposure to D12(176,58) negative and
D12(175,58) positive with a 3,260-vertex path. It explicitly fails strict
blind outcome isolation. Its earlier two-ray argument predates exposure;
it reports not using those outcomes as premises/design/test selection.
The same-verifier arithmetic rereading is not another reviewer.

Neither route is Lean-formalized or human proof-reviewed. The eighteen
claim records, their two-route dependencies, status values and empty
Lean lists are unchanged. The classification theorem deliberately keeps
the single word `received` to preserve its full text byte-for-byte; this
is the one process-term exception in a protected theorem, not a new receipt.
The implementation claim IDs containing `route_b` are preserved machine
references; their prose headings and bibliography use neutral role names.

## Reproduction provenance and technical limits

The coordinator's 21 October summary is the primary reproduction
record: five executors exit zero, six mathematical comparisons are
EXACT_MATCH, and nineteen source hashes are supplied. Eighteen match the
submitted commit; the only difference is the later `mac_memory_guard.py`
revision parameterizing the inventory timeout and check interval.
Its bytes match the cited later commit. This is the coordinator's run
and research-side static readback, not a new research execution.

Historical T0250 has sixteen of nineteen hashes matching the submitted
commit. The differing `d12_affine_certificate.py`, `d12_affine2.py` and
`d12_affine3.py` are described by RouteB as summary-output argument changes.
The exact old source bytes are unavailable in Git; that explanation is
attributed and not directly verified. The old hashes and qualification
remain preserved. Two unchanged research-side replay attempts stopped
at the read-only `lsof` inventory timeout before any executor started.
The memory guard was not bypassed. This is technical failure, neither
reproduction nor refutation of the mathematics.

The v0 thirteen-step reproduction regenerates seven JSON summaries with
runtime fields ignored. It uses the supplied executor and is not an
independent soundness checker. Its maximum was about 239.1 MiB under an
active 2 GiB group-plus-driver limit. Known finite graphs, canonical
start events and arithmetic readbacks overlap. Earlier discovery/tests
had nine points beyond their prose r,s<=400 bound (r up to 441, s up to 453)
and a reported 3,037,282,304-byte RSS exceeding the 2 GB target. The error
and source history are preserved. Wedge tests q=25..30 were not executed;
they are not evidence for an unbounded tail. Successful-start counts,
including the 96 count at r=1,s>=3, remain auxiliary predictions.

Route A's JSON summaries are not full proof traces with all implication
witnesses. Their six semantic obligations remain mathematical premises.
Route B's rational certificate arithmetic is independently checkable from
its weights, while the long-strip geometry, quotient correspondence,
global simplicity and unbounded induction remain prose obligations.
The external cyclic-edge-connectivity result is cited as known; its
original proof has not been newly audited. Cited researchers have not
endorsed the work; novelty review is bounded and unresolved.
